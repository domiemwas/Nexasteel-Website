WHAT WE DO

From Concept to Installation.

NexaSteel provides end-to-end support for advertising structures and steel fabrication projects.

01

Design & Engineering

We plan advertising structures around the required dimensions, installation environment, visibility requirements and structural considerations.

02

Steel Fabrication

We fabricate billboard frames, rooftop structures, wall-mounted systems, signboard frames and other custom steel advertising structures.

03

Branding & Printing

We support the preparation and installation of branded advertising surfaces so completed structures are ready to communicate your business clearly.

04

Transport

Completed structures and fabricated components can be transported to the project location for final installation.

05

Installation

We install advertising structures on-site, including roadside, rooftop, wall-mounted and business-premises applications.

06

Maintenance

We provide maintenance support to help keep structures secure, presentable and suitable for continued use.
